Microsoft, Apple, and Discord have no shared app, so they need your own `client_id` and `client_secret`. Apple and Discord work only on Better Auth projects, not legacy Stack Auth. For Apple, `client_id` is the Services ID, `client_secret` is the `.p8` private key, and `apple_team_id` and `apple_key_id` are required.
